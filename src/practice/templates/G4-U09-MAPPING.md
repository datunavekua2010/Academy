## Detailed Specifications

### 1. მონეტების დათვლა (Counting Coins)
- `g4-u09-01` (Level 1): How many 10-tetri coins make 1 GEL?
- `g4-u09-02` (Level 1): How many 50-tetri coins make N GEL?
- `g4-u09-03` (Level 2): Summing multiple coins (50t, 20t, 10t, 5t) into total tetri.
- `g4-u09-04` (Level 2): 2-GEL and 1-GEL coins combined sum in GEL.
- `g4-u09-05` (Level 3): Expressing mixed coins in GEL and tetri format.
- `g4-u09-06` (Level 3): Determining smallest number of coins needed to pay exact change.

### 2. კუპიურების დათვლა (Counting Banknotes)
