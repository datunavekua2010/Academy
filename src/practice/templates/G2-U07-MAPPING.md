# Grade 2 — Unit 07: რაოდენობის მოდელები (Quantity Models)
## Template Mapping & Architecture Documentation

### Overview
Unit 07 covers foundational quantity representation models for Grade 2 mathematics in Georgian. It includes 6 subtopics and 60 authored templates (Q01–Q60), providing diverse representations using emoji rows, dot arrays, base-10 blocks (tens rods and ones units), shaded 10- and 20-frame grids, multi-way decomposition, and arithmetic with models.
